ABOUT FACULTY IN VARDHAMAN COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

The faculty of Vardhaman College of Engineering consists of highly qualified and experienced professors, associate professors, and assistant professors dedicated to academic excellence. Many faculty members hold doctoral degrees and are actively involved in research, publications, consultancy projects, and professional development activities. Their expertise helps students gain both theoretical knowledge and practical understanding of engineering concepts.

The faculty members employ modern teaching methodologies, including interactive lectures, project-based learning, laboratory experiments, and digital learning tools. They continuously update their knowledge through workshops, conferences, faculty development programs, and industry collaborations. This ensures that students receive education that is aligned with current technological trends and industry requirements.

Apart from teaching, the faculty plays an important role in mentoring students for academic success, research activities, internships, and career development. They encourage participation in technical competitions, innovation projects, and extracurricular activities. Through their guidance and support, the faculty contributes significantly to the overall growth and professional development of students at Vardhaman College of Engineering.
