INTRODUCTION ABOUT VARDHAMAN COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Vardhaman College of Engineering (VCE) is a premier engineering institution located in Kacharam, Shamshabad, Hyderabad, Telangana. Established in 1999 by the Vardhaman Educational Society, the college is a UGC Autonomous institution affiliated with JNTUH and approved by AICTE. VCE is accredited with an NAAC A++ Grade and several of its programs are accredited by the NBA (Tier-I), reflecting its commitment to high academic standards and quality education.

The college offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, technology, and management. It emphasizes student-centered learning, research, innovation, and industry-oriented education. VCE is equipped with modern laboratories, digital classrooms, a well-stocked library, hostels, sports facilities, and various student clubs that support both academic and extracurricular development.

Vardhaman College of Engineering has a strong placement record and attracts many reputed recruiters from the IT and core engineering sectors. The institution provides training programs, coding practice sessions, internships, and career guidance to enhance students' employability. Companies such as Amazon, Infosys, Cognizant, Accenture, Capgemini, and TCS regularly recruit from the campus, making VCE one of the well-regarded engineering colleges in Telangana.
