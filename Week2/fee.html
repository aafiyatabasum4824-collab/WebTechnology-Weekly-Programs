FEE STRUCTURE AT VARDHAMAN COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

The fee structure at Vardhaman College of Engineering varies depending on the course, admission category, and government regulations. For B.Tech programs, students admitted through the Telangana EAMCET counseling process generally pay the tuition fee prescribed by the Telangana State Government, while management quota admissions may have different fee structures.

Apart from the tuition fee, students may need to pay additional charges such as examination fees, library fees, laboratory fees, transportation fees, hostel fees (if applicable), and other miscellaneous charges. The college provides detailed fee information during the admission process to ensure transparency and clarity for students and parents.

Eligible students can also avail themselves of government scholarships and fee reimbursement schemes offered by the Telangana Government, subject to the prescribed eligibility criteria. These financial assistance programs help reduce the educational expenses of deserving students and support their academic journey at the institution.
